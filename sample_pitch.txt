PROJECT TITLE: Echoes of Silence
GENRE: Sci-Fi Thriller
LOGLINE: A solitary researcher on a remote island discovers signals from a dead civilization.

STORY SYNOPSIS:
Dr. Aris lives alone in a decaying weather station on a northern island. When his radio equipment begins intercepting localized voice loops from 50 years ago, he realizes the island holds a forgotten research facility beneath the cliffs. 

As storms isolate him completely from the mainland, he descends into the bunkers to find who is broadcasting—only to realize the signals are predicting his own actions in real time.

He must navigate the flooded subterranean corridors to shut down the transmitter before the rising tide seals the bunker permanently.
