LUNIM FILM SUITE — REGIONAL INDIE FILM GRANT & INVESTOR COMPLIANCE MANDATES

1. ELIGIBILITY & TEAM STRUCTURE
- Core Team: Must have a designated Lead Director, Producer, and Screenwriter attached.
- Chain of Title: Full legal ownership or option agreement for the script/IP must be documented.
- Experience Tier: At least one core producer or director must have a completed short film or feature credit.

2. BUDGETING & FINANCIAL CLEARANCE
- Maximum Grant Funding Cap: Up to £150,000 or 50% of total estimated production budget.
- Co-Financing Requirement: Remaining 50% must be secured via pre-sales, tax credits, or equity partners.
- Contingency Allocation: A mandatory 10% contingency line item must be explicitly reserved in the budget breakdown.
- Escrow Compliance: All allocated grant distributions are subject to milestone-based release via Lunim Smart Contracts.

3. SCRIPT, LOGLINE & MARKET FEASIBILITY
- Logline & Synopsis: Must include a clear 1-sentence logline and a 3-paragraph story treatment.
- Audience Positioning: Must identify a primary target demographic and a clear direct-to-fan distribution strategy (e.g., Tabb community adoption).
- Production Feasibility: Detailed breakdown of shoot locations, shooting days (recommended max 25 days), and technical complexity.

4. DIVERSITY, EQUITY & SUSTAINABILITY MANDATES
- On-Set Inclusivity: Production crew must meet minimum diversity quotas across head-of-department roles.
- Green Production: Must outline a carbon footprint reduction plan for location shooting and travel.
