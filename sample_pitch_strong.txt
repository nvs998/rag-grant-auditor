PROJECT TITLE: The Iron Horizon
GENRE: Historical Drama / Thriller
LOGLINE: In 1942 Scotland, a female cryptanalyst stationed at a coastal radar site uncovers an enemy sabotage plot within her own unit.

STORY SYNOPSIS:
In mid-1942, Eleanor Vance works at a remote radar array on the Scottish coastline tracking naval threats across the North Sea. When she notices deliberate mathematical anomalies in transmitted signal logs, she realizes a covert operative is feeding corrupted vector data to incoming Allied convoys.

Isolated by severe Atlantic gales and unable to contact headquarters, Eleanor must uncover the insider's identity among a small crew of engineers before an approaching convoy is guided directly into an enemy submarine trap.

Working against shifting loyalties and limited time, she uses mechanical signal decoders to expose the betrayer and successfully reroute the convoy to safety.
